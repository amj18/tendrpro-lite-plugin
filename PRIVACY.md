# TendrPro Lite Skills Pack — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

The TendrPro Lite skills pack is a set of instruction files for Claude.
It contains no code, scripts, hooks, MCP servers, analytics or
telemetry.

- **No data collection.** The publisher (amj18) does not receive,
  collect, store or process any data from people who use the skills.
- **Files you provide** (RFPs, supplier proposals, knowledge-base
  documents) are read only by Claude within your own session, under
  your Claude account's terms and privacy settings.
- **Outputs** are written only to locations you choose, within your
  own session or workspace.
- **Nothing is sent** to the publisher or any third party. Email
  drafts are produced as text and are never sent.

Your use of Claude itself is governed by Anthropic's privacy policy.

Questions: open an issue at
